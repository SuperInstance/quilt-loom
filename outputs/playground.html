01 — the adversarial loom

Classic GANs train a forger against a judge. Here the forger writes logic and the judge owns probes. The discriminator's arithmetic — compile, probe, score — runs inside quilt program cells; listeners crown, scar, and harden as reflexes. The tension is real: every fake the generator slips through makes the forge harder; every scar the forge takes narrows the generator's escape routes.
